Atlas — global news intelligence

Atlas helps people inspect how the same current news story is covered across the world. It starts with real source articles, clusters reports only when the evidence supports a same-event relationship, and shows where participating outlets have their primary editorial markets. Regional coverage never means reader exposure. Audience and readership telemetry are outside the product. Event location, publisher origin, outlet language, and primary editorial market remain separate facts with visible confidence and evidence.

Select a place to inspect current stories connected to events there, then select one story to compare coverage by other outlets. The focused view shows the story's evidenced editorial-market coverage heat, source-normalized and raw prominence, article citations, and any supported differences in claims or framing. Atlas leaves tone, contradiction, omission, and geography unknown when the available evidence cannot support a conclusion; it never fills gaps with demo news or invented comparisons.

The public service is free and read-only. Its browser explorer and machine interfaces share the same current truth store. No sign-in, OAuth flow, API key, payment, account, mutation, or credential-management operation is exposed. Automated clients should preserve source URLs, uncertainty, timestamps, and the distinction between editorial market and event location when presenting Atlas results.
